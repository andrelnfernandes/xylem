## What you get

<div class="features">
<div class="feature">
<h3>Open almost anything right there</h3>
<p>SQLite with full <code>JOIN</code> queries, spreadsheets, CSV, Parquet, JSON, Markdown, logs and archives.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Turn data into files</h3>
<p>One table becomes a page, a SQL script or a database, with a preview before anything is written.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Free up space</h3>
<p>See what takes room, and find duplicate files and folders, even on Google Drive without downloading them.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Reach beyond your disk</h3>
<p>Browse and copy from servers over SSH, and work with Google Drive, S3 and other clouds through rclone.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Stay in one place</h3>
<p>A built-in terminal whose tree follows your <code>cd</code>, and Git status right in the file list.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Open files you don't trust</h3>
<p>Nothing in them runs, and everything has a limit.</p>
</div>
<div class="feature">
<h3>Make it yours</h3>
<p>Twelve themes, configurable keys, and help in six languages.</p>
</div>
</div>
